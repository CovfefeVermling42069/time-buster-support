Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Time Buster does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works entirely on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves one setting on your device only: whether sound is muted. This setting never leaves your device.

What the app does not do

Time Buster has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and does not connect to the internet.

Children

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it is safe for use by people of all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
